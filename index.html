Because if I've had a horrible day, if everything feels too heavy, or if I'm in a room full of people but still need someone who feels familiar, you're one of the people I'd want to run to. You're someone I want to tell things to. Someone I want to share the little moments with, not just the big ones. Someone whose presence can matter even when we're not actively talking.

Somehow, you became home to me.

Not because distance doesn't exist, or because friendship is always easy. But because you became someone who feels like family to me. Someone I feel connected to even across all those kilometres. Someone who found a place in my heart without needing to be physically beside me to earn it.

It's still a little unbelievable to me that a person I met so randomly on the internet could become this important. I didn't plan it. I didn't know it was happening. One day you were someone I was getting to know, and then, without any grand announcement, you were my Sistaaaa. My bestie. My family.

I'm so glad our paths crossed. Out of all the people we could have met, all the conversations we could have had, somehow ours happened. And if I could go back to that beginning already knowing how much you'd come to mean to me, I'd still want our paths to cross.

For this new chapter of your life, I hope beautiful things find their way to you. I hope you get opportunities that make you excited about your future, people who appreciate you properly, moments that make you laugh until your face hurts, and days that remind you how much there is to look forward to.

I hope your dreams have room to grow. I hope you get to be unapologetically yourself. I hope the world gives you reasons to feel proud of who you are, and that even on the ordinary days, you remember that your existence matters beyond what you do for anyone else.

You deserve happiness that doesn't need to be earned through exhaustion. You deserve kindness, laughter, love, wonderful surprises, and a whole year full of little things that make life feel brighter.

And I wish I could be there in person to celebrate you today. But even from far away, I am celebrating you. I'm celebrating the day you were born, the person you are, and the fact that I get to call you my Sistaaaa.

Thank you for being the first online friend who became so much more than I expected. Thank you for becoming someone I feel safe with, someone who feels like family, and someone I can imagine running to after a difficult day, even if there are miles between us.

Out of all the strangers in the world, I found you. And I'm so glad I did. ♡

Happy birthday, Relebohile. I hope this year is as wonderful as I want it to be for you, and then somehow even better.

All my love, always,
